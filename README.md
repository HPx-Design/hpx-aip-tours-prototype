# All-In Plan onboarding prototype

An interactive prototype of the All-In Plan journey: the welcome email, app onboarding, printer setup, the printer feature tour, a jump ahead three weeks, and the All-In Plan tips.

**Open the prototype:** [https://hpx-design.github.io/hpx-aip-tours-prototype](https://hpx-design.github.io/hpx-aip-tours-prototype/)

## Test on your phone (recommended)

The prototype runs full screen, like the real app, when you add it to your Home Screen.

### iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**. Add to Home Screen only works from Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open it from the new **AIP prototype** icon.

### Android

1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮** (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Add**.
4. Open it from the new **AIP prototype** icon.

### Tips

- **Start over:** close the app from your app switcher and open it again.
- **Seeing an old version?** Delete the icon from your Home Screen and add it again.
- Opening the link in a regular browser tab works too, but the browser's address bar and toolbar will show.

## Test on a computer

Open the link in Chrome, Safari or Edge. The panel on the left lets you:

- Jump to any part of the journey
- Go **Back** or **Restart**
- Turn on **Show tap areas** to see everything you can tap

## How to move through it

- Tap the buttons and cards you'd tap in the real app.
- Tap anywhere that isn't tappable to see where you can tap.
- After the feature tour, tap **Jump ahead 3 weeks** to skip to the All-In Plan tips.
- Swipe the tip cards left and right, or use **Next tip** and **Back**.
